HOW TO PLAY

Build absurd teams of fictional characters, then win each round with both votes and contextual intel scoring.

  • Lobby: Join room (3-6 players)
  • Rounds 1-3: Draft 2 characters per scenario, then score via votes + AI intel fit
  • Plot Twist: Surprise modifier revealed
  • Voting: Vote for best team (can change before locking!)
  • Round 4: Final AI showdown evaluates your full 6-character roster
  • ✓ You can change votes until you lock
  • ✓ Auto-filled characters fill duplicates
  • ✓ Lock early to speed up the game
  • ✓ Context matters: mystery rounds favor character/person picks over generic objects
  • ✓ Press ENTER to submit drafts
