📚 HOW TO PLAY 📚

🎯 The Goal

Build absurd teams of fictional characters and vote on whose team would win each scenario!

🎮 Game Flow

  • Lobby: Join a room with 3-6 players, get ready
  • Rounds 1-3: Draft 2 characters per round for each scenario
  • Plot Twist: A surprise modifier changes how teams are judged
  • Voting: Vote on which team would win (You can change votes before locking!)
  • Results: See points earned and running leaderboard
  • Final Round: Assemble your best 6-character team from all previous picks

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • ENTER - Submit character during draft
  • ESC - Access help/menu (future feature)
  • Note: All votes must be LOCKED to count!

⚡ Pro Tips

  • You can change your vote until you click LOCK
  • Auto-filled characters appear when duplicates happen
  • Locking early helps the game move faster
  • The final round rewards big teams!
